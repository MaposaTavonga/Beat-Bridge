Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Beat Bridge has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking. Your beats stay on your own device.

What stays on your device

Your patterns, lesson progress and settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What other services see

  • Hosting: the company that hosts this site keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver the page and stop abuse.
  • Fonts: the page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • AI coach: the coach only runs in the Claude version of Beat Bridge, on your own Claude account. There, your questions and the state of your beat are sent to Anthropic under your Claude account's terms. Nothing from this site is sent to an AI service.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask what personal information we hold about you. In practice we hold none, but you can contact us any time.
